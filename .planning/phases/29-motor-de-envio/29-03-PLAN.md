## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Admin cancel action → in-flight scheduled jobs | `Divulgacao#cancelar!` (phase 28) only flips a DB column — it never touches already-enqueued `solid_queue` rows. The entire DIVU-08 guarantee rests on every `SendToGroupJob#perform` re-checking `divulgacao.reload.status_cancelada?` at execution time, not at enqueue time. |
| Queue capacity → ActionCable broadcast latency | INFRA-06's whole purpose is protecting v1.5's synchronous ActionCable broadcasts from being starved by WhatsApp send volume — RESEARCH confirms broadcasts today are synchronous (not job-based), so there is no CURRENT contention, but the dedicated worker is the preventive guard for future job-based broadcasts. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-29-09 | Repudiation | Cancel-after-enqueue race window | medium | mitigate | Every `SendToGroupJob#perform` re-reads `divulgacao.status` fresh from the DB (`divulgacao.reload`) at execution time, immediately before the atomic claim — a cancel committed at any point before that reload is always observed; proven by the mid-dispatch cancellation test (Task 2). |
| T-29-10 | Denial of Service | A Divulgação with many groups saturating the shared job queue | low | accept | `SEND_DELAY_MIN/MAX` (ENV-driven, phase 28) enforces a floor on inter-group spacing regardless of group count; `whatsapp_sends` gets dedicated worker capacity (INFRA-06) so a large Divulgação's staggered sends cannot starve unrelated jobs on the `"*"` queue. No per-Divulgação group-count cap is introduced here — out of scope per CONTEXT.md (anti-ban posture is ENV-driven conservatism, not a feature of this phase). |
| T-29-11 | Availability | divulgacoes.status stuck in em_andamento forever | medium | mitigate | `finalize_divulgacao_if_done` is called from every exit path of `SendToGroupJob#perform` (inline guards in 29-01, taxonomy discard_on/retry_on-exhaustion blocks in 29-02) — proven under a multi-group scenario in Task 2 that the divulgação always reaches `concluida` exactly when the last pendente item resolves, through any combination of success/failure/incerto outcomes. |
